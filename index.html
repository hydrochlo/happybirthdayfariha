30 October 2026

Dear Mouri,

Happy Birthday!

Let me get the awkward part out of the way: I’m late. I know. Everyone else had already finished their wishes, and I turned up with mine when the cake was half gone. You made me earn my way in here with a forgiveness password, so I’m treating “I forgive you for late wish” as legally binding. No take-backs.

In my defence, I wanted to give you something better than a quick text. So I made this instead. That is my excuse, and I’m sticking to it.

I’m really glad you’re one of my best friends. Some people make a day feel lighter just by being in it, and you’re one of them. I don’t say that nearly often enough. Thank you for the laughs, for the patience, and for being someone I can be myself around without editing anything.

Here is what I wish for you this year. Good health, first of all, because nothing else works without it. Peace of mind, the kind that lets you sleep well and stop replaying conversations from three days ago. Success in the things you’re working toward, and the courage to start the ones you’re still only thinking about. And plenty of new places, new food and good experiences that turn into stories later.

You deserve good things, Mouri. Not someday, and not after you’ve earned them twice. Now, on ordinary days too. If you ever forget that, come back to this page.

So eat the cake. Take too many photos. Ignore your phone for an hour, after you finish reading this, of course.

Happy Birthday again, and sorry again for being late. I’m looking forward to making it up to you.

With love,
Your (very late) friend
